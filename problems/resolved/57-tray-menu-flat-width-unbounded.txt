57 LOW tray menu flat-width unbounded

   file: src/bar/tray/watcher.rs::get_menu_tree::parse_item

   why:  Finding 34 (batch 8) capped recursion depth at 16 but not
         per-level width. A single GetLayout response with 1M flat
         children allocates 1M TrayMenuItem (~100MB) before the depth
         guard can fire — user-triggered (click tray icon → context
         menu). Requires 128MB D-Bus message, which is the practical
         ceiling.

   fix:  MAX_ITEMS_PER_LEVEL = 1000 (break before recursion into next
         child). Combined with depth 16: worst case = 16 × 1000 = 16K
         items in memory regardless of tree shape.
