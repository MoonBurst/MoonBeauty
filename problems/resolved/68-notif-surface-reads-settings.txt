68 CRITICAL notif-surface monitor routing broken by dual-spawn-path race

   file: src/main.rs (is_notif_surface block)
         src/app/update.rs (PollNotifs spawn)

   why:  Two spawn paths raced for the org.freedesktop.Notifications
         name claim:
           (a) bar-spawn: Command::spawn() with MBS_OUTPUT from settings
           (b) D-Bus activation / systemd-user: no MBS_OUTPUT, defaults
               to primary DP-2
         Whichever won the claim determined the monitor. Loser exited
         → zombie. Over a session: 8 zombies accumulated as bar
         children, and the LIVE notif-surface was the D-Bus-spawned
         one on DP-2, ignoring notif_screen_name entirely.

   fix:  notif-surface re-reads settings.json at startup, overriding
         MBS_OUTPUT from notif_screen_name. Parent env (bar, D-Bus,
         systemd) is now irrelevant — every spawner produces a child
         that resolves the same disk value.

   verified: monitor-change test shows MBS_OUTPUT matching
             notif_screen_name, toast appears on configured monitor,
             zombie count drains on bar restart.
