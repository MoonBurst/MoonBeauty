35 LOW-DoS lockscreen buffer unbounded (via rogue peer)

   file: src/overlays/lockscreen/coordinator.rs (LockIpc::Key handler)

   why:  Key(s) appends every char of s to the shared buffer without
         cap. Rogue same-user peer sends Key("A".repeat(10000)) →
         coordinator broadcasts BufferUpdate(10000) → every peer's UI
         renders 10000 dots. Cosmetic (dots not real password chars)
         but saturates the render loop.

   fix:  MAX_BUF = 256 (real passwords ≤128 chars; 256 is generous
         headroom for IME composition), MAX_PER_KEY = 64. Both caps
         applied per-message. Excess chars silently dropped.

   residual:  Rogue peer can still hammer the coordinator with
              Key(1-char) × 10000 — each hits the buffer cap and gets
              dropped, but the broadcast still fires per message. Cost
              per message is <10μs; 10000 messages = 100ms of CPU.
              Acceptable.
