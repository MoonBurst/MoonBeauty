34 HIGH-DoS dbusmenu parse_item unbounded recursion

   file: src/bar/tray/watcher.rs::get_menu_tree::parse_item

   why:  parse_item recurses through dbusmenu children with no depth
         limit. A hostile same-user tray app returns a menu tree
         nested 10000-deep → ~2 MB stack per 10 levels → SIGSEGV at
         ~1000 levels → shell crash. Killing the shell also kills the
         lockscreen child watchdog → screen unlocked until respawn.

   fix:  MAX_DEPTH = 16. Recursion guard at function entry; truncation
         logged. Real dbusmenu trees are 2-4 levels.

   residual:  Recursion still happens, just bounded. Truncated entries
              are dropped silently (log fires). Acceptable — hostile
              apps lose menu items but can't crash the shell.
