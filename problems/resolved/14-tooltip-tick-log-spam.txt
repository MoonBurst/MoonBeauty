14 LOW hot-path eprintln in tooltip Tick handlers
   file: src/overlays/system_tooltip.rs (Message::Tick)
         src/overlays/info_tooltip.rs
         src/bar/weather/tooltip.rs
         src/bar/tray/popup.rs
   why:  Each tooltip's Tick handler logs:
           eprintln!("[tick] frame={} elapsed={}ms", ...);
         While a tooltip is animating (first ~2s), ticks are 4ms apart,
         so ~250 lines/sec to stderr. Over a full animation this is a
         few hundred lines per tooltip. The systemd user service
         forwards stderr to journalctl, so this pollutes the journal
         and can rotate out useful messages.
   fix:  Gate behind env var (std::env::var_os("MBS_TICK_DIAG")) or
         remove entirely. Audit for similar per-frame eprintlns in the
         same handlers.
