49 LOW IPC slowloris (accept loop block)

   file: src/app/new_app.rs (main IPC accept loop)
         src/overlays/screenshot/ipc.rs (annotator IPC accept loop)

   why:  3s read timeout per connection meant a same-user process
         sending nothing blocked the single-threaded accept loop for
         3s per connection. N connections = N × 3s of blocked IPC.

   fix:  1s read timeout (20x a healthy round-trip).
