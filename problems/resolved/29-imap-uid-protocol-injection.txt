29 HIGH IMAP uid protocol injection (sibling of finding 28)

   file: src/libraries/backend/email_client.rs
         ::fetch_raw_message, ::toggle_star, ::move_to_trash,
         ::move_to_spam, ::mark_not_spam, ::delete_message
         ::load_index (producer filter)

   why:  async-imap's `uid_fetch`, `uid_store`, `uid_copy` embed the
         uid verbatim in the wire command. A crafted `id` from a
         poisoned index.json (hand-edited, or written by a build
         predating this fix) can inject:
             uid_fetch("1\r\nDELETE INBOX", "(BODY.PEEK[])")
         Same severity as finding 28 (folder argument), same class of
         exploit — arbitrary IMAP command execution on next open.

   fix:  is_safe_imap_uid() — non-empty, ≤20 bytes, all ASCII digits.
         Server-assigned uids are u32 (1–10 digits). Defense-in-depth:
           1. load_index: retain-only filter on read
           2. Every uid_* caller: early Err before session call

   residual:  uid_expunge has no id (operates on the whole SELECT'd
              folder). No injection surface there.
