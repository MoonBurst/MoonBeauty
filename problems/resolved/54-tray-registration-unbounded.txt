54 MED tray watcher registration unbounded

   file: src/bar/tray/watcher.rs::register_status_notifier_item

   why:  RegisterStatusNotifierItem accepted arbitrary strings with no
         length or count cap. A same-user process could loop with unique
         strings; HashSet<String> growth OOMs the bar. Killing the bar
         also kills lockscreen child watchdog → unlock window.

   fix:  MAX_SERVICE_LEN = 250 (bounds per-entry cost), MAX_ITEMS = 500
         (bounds total). Reject silent (return Ok without insert) so a
         hostile client learns nothing.

   residual:  A patient attacker can still register 500 items, then
              wait for TTL expiry and re-register. Bounded at 500 ×
              250 bytes = 125 KB, which is negligible.
