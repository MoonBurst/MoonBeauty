15 LOW save() logs tooltip_sizes on every write
   file: src/libraries/config/settings.rs::ShellSettings::save
   why:  Every call to save() runs:
           eprintln!("[save] writing tooltip_sizes={:?} bar_monitor={:?}",
                     self.tooltip_sizes, self.bar_monitor);
         Save is rate-limited to 300ms in the settings UI, so dragging a
         slider emits ~3 lines/sec. The map is often hundreds of bytes,
         so log volume is non-trivial.
   fix:  Remove or gate behind MBS_SETTINGS_DEBUG. For developers,
         prefer a #[cfg(debug_assertions)] variant so the line cannot
         fire in release builds.
