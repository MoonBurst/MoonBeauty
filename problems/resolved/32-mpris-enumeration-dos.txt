32 HIGH-DoS MPRIS player enumeration unbounded iteration

   file: src/libraries/backend/mpris.rs::get_active_player, ::poll

   why:  Iterates all org.mpris.MediaPlayer2.* bus names, calling
         proxy.get_property::<String>("PlaybackStatus").await for each.
         A hostile app can register thousands of names (evil1..evil10000);
         each call blocks until D-Bus default timeout (25s in zbus 4.x).
         Worst case: 10000 × 25s = 70 hours per poll — permanent hang.

   fix:  Three-layer bound:
           1. .take(20) on mpris_names — real systems have ≤5 players
           2. tokio::time::timeout(2s) per get_property — 40s worst case
           3. tokio::time::timeout(10s) around poll_uncached — hard cap
         Plus: cache None results for 30s (vs 5s for Some) so a hostile
         player that triggers timeout isn't polled again on the next
         hardware tick.

   residual:  A hostile player can still cause one 10s hang per 30s
              window. Bounded but non-zero. True fix needs a
              "remember-and-skip bad players" list, out of scope.
