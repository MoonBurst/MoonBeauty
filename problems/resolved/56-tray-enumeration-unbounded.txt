56 MED tray item enumeration unbounded iteration

   file: src/bar/tray/watcher.rs::fetch_tray_items

   why:  Chained from finding 54 — a hostile process that registered N
         names drives N Proxy::new calls. Each blocks up to 25s (D-Bus
         default) on timeout; the fetcher runs every 15s. N=1000 means
         ~7 hours per fetch cycle.

   fix:  Two-layer bound:
           - .take(200) on raw_items (real systems have ≤20 SNI items)
           - 3s tokio::time::timeout per Proxy::new
         Worst-case: 200 * 3s = 10 min per fetch, still slow but the
         next fetch queues rather than stacking.

   residual:  10 min per fetch is still slow. A "remember-and-skip bad
              peers" list would close it fully; cost > benefit for a
              same-user attack surface.
