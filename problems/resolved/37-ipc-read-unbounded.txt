37 LOW IPC read_to_end unbounded

   file: src/app/new_app.rs (inline accept loop)
         src/libraries/ipc/protocol.rs::IpcServer::accept

   why:  read_to_end on the socket with no size cap. Same-user client
         sending 1 GB of JSON-shaped bytes → 1 GB allocation → OOM.

   fix:  Take(4 MB). Truncated data fails JSON parse → Err returned
         → connection handled gracefully. Valid IpcCommands are
         ≤~100 KB (biggest is IncomingNotif{app_name,summary,body}).

   residual:  Attacker can still cause the accept loop to churn
              through rejections at TCP speed. Bounded by the 3s read
              timeout + single-threaded accept. Acceptable.
