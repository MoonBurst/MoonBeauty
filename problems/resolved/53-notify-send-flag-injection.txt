53 LOW-preventive notify-send args without -- guard

   file: src/app/update.rs (3 call sites)
         src/main.rs (--notify CLI path)

   why:  notify-send's getopt parser treats leading-dash args as flags.
         Sender/subject from attacker-controlled IMAP could look like
         --urgency=critical or --icon=/path. Nuisance only — no exploit
         class, but institutionalize the pattern.

   fix:  Insert `--` between options and positional arguments at all 4
         sites. Positional args (sender, subject) can't be reinterpreted.
